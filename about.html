About

Hi, I'm the one behind @mapofthesaints

Lean Luggage started as a way to write down what didn't fit in a 60-second video — the exact address, the honest cost, the reason we went the way most people don't. If you found this site through TikTok, welcome; the destinations and itineraries here are the long version of what's on the feed.

The whole idea is simple: everyone's seen the postcard shot. We'd rather find what's down the road from it — the childhood home instead of the death house, the doodle farm instead of the overlook, the town three stops before the famous one.

How we choose what goes on the site

A place makes it into Hidden Gems only after we've been there ourselves and checked it against the well-known version nearby. If it isn't meaningfully quieter, more honest, or more worth the extra effort, it doesn't get the stamp.
