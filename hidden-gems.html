Aberdeen, Washington

Not the house where he died — the one where he grew up

Everyone visits the Seattle house. The Aberdeen childhood home and the Wishkah River — where his ashes were scattered — get almost no visitors, despite telling the more honest version of the story.

Quiet, residential, respectful visits only. No plaque, no crowd.

Vermont

The golden doodle farm, not the leaf-peeping overlook

The classic Vermont fall photo is the same overlook everyone's Instagram has. Twenty minutes off that route, a working doodle farm makes for a stranger, better afternoon — and a much better photo.

Call ahead — small working farm, not a tourist attraction.

Connecticut

The real, hard-to-reach Stars Hollow

The town that inspired Gilmore Girls' Stars Hollow isn't easy to get to and isn't set up for tourists — which is exactly why it still feels real.

No train station nearby; this one needs a car and a plan.
