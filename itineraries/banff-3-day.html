Itinerary · 3 Days · Canada

The Weekend Rockies Run

Banff and Lake Louise in a long weekend, timed to beat the parking-lot crowds and skip nothing that matters.

3 daysfits a long weekend
Shoulder seasonbest value window
Car neededrental or your own

Day 1 — Town + Sulphur Mountain

Land, settle into Banff town, and use the afternoon light for the Sulphur Mountain gondola or the hiking trail up it if your legs are willing. Dinner in town before the weekend crowds arrive.

Book Lake Louise parking or the shuttle the night before — the lot fills before 7am most of the year.

Day 2 — Lake Louise & Moraine Lake, early

Arrive at Lake Louise before sunrise. It sounds extreme until you see the parking lot at 9am. Walk the lakeshore trail, then catch the shuttle to Moraine Lake for the view everyone's seen a photo of, minus the photo's crowd. Afternoon free for the Plain of Six Glaciers trail if you've got the legs left.

Day 3 — The ridge nobody stops for

On the way out of the park, most guides send you straight to the airport. Don't. The pull-off 20 minutes past the main viewpoints has the same peaks, an empty shoulder to park on, and nobody else there. Ten minutes, free, no ticket required.

Budget shape

Split a rental car three or four ways, cook breakfasts in the room, and the biggest single cost becomes the one splurge dinner — which is exactly how we'd plan it again.
